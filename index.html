What I build

Every business needs something different from its site. A few examples of what goes into it.

  • Cafés and restaurants

    Menu with photos and prices, table booking, delivery links, a map that opens in the phone’s navigator.

  • Clinics and beauty salons

    Services with prices, the team, online booking or a WhatsApp button, reviews.

  • Shops and catalogs

    Products with filters, ordering through WhatsApp or online, delivery and returns spelled out.

  • Builders and home services

    A cost calculator, finished jobs with photos, a request form that reaches you in seconds.

  • Furniture and manufacturing

    A product configurator with a 3D preview and a price that updates as people choose.

  • Hotels and venues

    Rooms or halls, dates, direct booking without commission, an optional 3D tour.

  • Experts and personal brands

    Portfolio, services, courses, booking a call, a page that says who you are in ten seconds.

  • Products and startups

    A landing page with a 3D view of the product, a waitlist or pre-order, fast load on mobile.

About

I’m Tolybay Uakhit, a web designer and developer based in Istanbul. I build websites for small businesses of any kind, by hand: clean code that opens fast even on an inexpensive phone, and three.js when a 3D scene helps to sell.

Whatever your business is, I’ll sketch your homepage first, for free, so you can judge the idea before we agree on anything.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any. Shops, cafés, clinics, salons, builders, schools, studios, hotels, experts. If people look for you online, a good site helps.

Do I need 3D on my site?

Not always. Most businesses need a clear, fast site first. 3D is worth it when your product, place or process looks better when people can turn it around.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site is built for phones first, and 3D scenes get a light version for phones that can’t run them.

Do you write the texts?

I can tidy your texts and suggest a structure. You send the facts: prices, services, photos and contacts.

Tell me about your business.

Send a link to your site or a few lines about what you do. I’ll reply with ideas and a fixed price.
