About

I’m Tolybay Uakhit, a web developer based in Istanbul. I build every site by hand: three.js for the 3D, Blender for the models, and plain, fast code underneath, so the page opens quickly even on an inexpensive phone.

The two projects above are demos, made to show the approach on real kinds of businesses. If you have a place worth showing, I’ll sketch your homepage first, for free.

Do I need my own 3D model?

No. I model the scene in Blender from your photos, or make a stylised version of your building or product.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. The 3D is tuned for mobile, and phones that can’t run it get a light version of the same page.

Can you add 3D to my existing site?

Yes. The 3D hero is a block that fits into WordPress, Webflow, Framer or Shopify.

Do you write the texts?

I can tidy your texts and suggest a structure. You send the facts: prices, services, photos and contacts.

Tell me about your place.

Send a link to your site or a few lines about the business. I’ll reply with ideas and a fixed price.
