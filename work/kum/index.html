How sand coffee is made

  1. Into the cezve

    Finely ground beans, cold water and, if you like, sugar. Tell us sade, orta or şekerli.

  2. Through the sand

    The barista moves the cezve through sand heated to 200 °C. It warms the coffee evenly from every side.

  3. When the foam rises

    The moment the foam reaches the rim, it goes into your cup, foam first. With water and a piece of lokum.
